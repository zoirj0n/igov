# Competitiveness indexes

Managed by `scripts/sync-sources.sh`. Source: `data/sources.csv`.

| Source | URL | Coverage | Best use |
|---|---|---|---|
| World Economic Forum publications | https://www.weforum.org/reports/ | Global competitiveness and risk reports | Broad competitiveness context |
| IMD World Competitiveness Center | https://www.imd.org/centers/world-competitiveness-center/ | Country competitiveness rankings | Executive benchmarking |
| World Bank Doing Business archives | https://www.worldbank.org/en/programs/business-enabling-environment | Legacy business environment metrics | Historical regulatory comparison |
| Global Innovation Index | https://www.globalinnovationindex.org/ | Innovation and capability rankings | Innovation benchmarking |
| Transparency International CPI | https://www.transparency.org/en/cpi | Corruption perception rankings | Governance screening |
| Heritage Foundation Index of Economic Freedom | https://www.heritage.org/index/ | Economic freedom rankings | Market orientation screening |
| Fraser Institute Economic Freedom of the World | https://www.fraserinstitute.org/studies/economic-freedom | Policy and institutional freedom rankings | Institutional comparison |
| IMD Smart City Index | https://www.imd.org/smart-city-observatory/home/ | City-level smart city rankings | Urban quality benchmarking |
| IESE Cities in Motion Index | https://www.iese.edu/cities-in-motion/ | Multi-dimensional city ranking (governance, mobility, sustainability) | Comprehensive urban performance scoring |
| EIU Safe Cities Index | https://safecities.economist.com/ | City safety across digital, health, infrastructure, personal | Security benchmarking for urban engagement |
